# Dataset source

Original, unmodified CSV downloads from Employees and Payroll, version 3:

- https://databasesample.com/datasets/employees-and-payroll
- https://database-sample-website.s3.eu-north-1.amazonaws.com/datasets/employees-and-payroll/v3/csv/employees.csv
- https://database-sample-website.s3.eu-north-1.amazonaws.com/datasets/employees-and-payroll/v3/csv/payroll.csv

Downloaded October 19, 2026. The publisher identifies the dataset as synthetic and CC0 1.0 Public Domain. There are 500 employee rows and 600 payroll rows. Dates and amounts are preserved even when they do not represent realistic payroll. Source file SHA-256 hashes and independent totals are recorded in `tests/expected.json`.
